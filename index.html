How Depthgram works

Your photo stays on your device

Depthgram works out the depth of every pixel right in your browser. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is stored; close the tab and your photo is gone.

Look around inside it

The photo becomes a scene with real depth. Move the pointer to look around, or take it off the photo and the camera drifts on its own in a smooth, seamless loop. Everything tunes itself to the photo; the sliders are there when you want them.

See it in 3D, keep what you make

Watch it live through red/cyan paper glasses or a VR viewer. Then save a still, a depth map, a 3D image, or a short video that loops without a seam.
