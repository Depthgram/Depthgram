How Depthgram works

Depth is computed on your device

Depth Anything V2 runs inside the page, on WebGPU where the browser has it and WASM everywhere else. Your photo is never uploaded, because there is no server to upload it to.

Motion is locked to your display

The renderer measures the refresh rate and closes every camera sweep on a whole frame, so the loop never beats against the screen. Focus plane and motion range are read from the depth histogram, not guessed.

Exports you can actually use

PNG frames and depth maps, red/cyan anaglyphs, parallel side-by-side stereo pairs for a VR viewer, and MP4 or WebM clips cut to a whole number of sweeps so they loop without a seam.
